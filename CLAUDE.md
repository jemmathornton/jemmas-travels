## Where things are

- `data/trips.json`: one line per trip (id, title, from/to as `YYYY-MM` or `YYYY`, country codes, optional named places with lat/lon for pins).
- `data/countries.json`: name, continent and map point per country; `home` (GB, not counted as visited); countries per continent for the tracker (195 in all).
- `data/posts/<trip id>/<nn>.md`: one post per file, a header between `---` lines (title, days `1` or `2-4` or dates, place, photos = file names in `site/photos/<trip id>/`, or a number of placeholder frames; placeholder: yes) then her words, blank line between paragraphs. **Photo markers** on a line of their own place photos in the text: `[photo 2]`, `[photo 2 wide|left|right|tall]`, `[photos 3-5]`, `[photos 3-8 mosaic]`, caption after a colon; unplaced photos go in a gallery at the end (`postBody` in app.js; guide for her: `for-jemma/post-templates.md`). The Japan posts and the ten **Italy template posts** (21 Nov, her ask: one per photo layout) are placeholders.
- `imports/world-atlas-2.0.2/`: Natural Earth country shapes as downloaded (110m and 50m TopoJSON, ISC licence). `tools/make_world.py` (run with `python -I`) turns them into `site/js/world.js` (generated; 1:110m for world maps, 1:50m for zoomed maps; rings across 180° made continuous). `site/js/map.js` draws them in the Natural Earth projection.
- `tools/serve.py`: the local server (port 8041, this PC only, and only by the names localhost / 127.0.0.1; the `travels` preview runs it, and the family server on 8050 starts it and passes `/travels/api/*` on). Serves site/ and `/family/` (Jemma’s Journals’ shared code) and saves the Write page’s posts (`POST /api/post`, needs header `X-Travels: 1`): photos straightened, shrunk to 1920 px and saved as JPEG with no hidden data (no GPS); her words saved exactly; checks the data and rebuilds; on any problem nothing is kept (trips.json restored exactly). Opened as plain files, the Write page says to start the server.
- `tools/build.py`: builds `site/js/data.js` (**generated, never edit**) and stamps file fingerprints into `site/index.html`. Run after any change to data/ or site/. It runs `tools/check_data.py` first and refuses to build if data/ has problems (bad dates, unknown countries, markers past the last photo, unmarked text in a placeholder post…).
- `site/site.css`: one stylesheet for the chosen Old Atlas look (rewritten 21 Nov; the trial looks are gone; `for-jemma/round-*` pictures show them). `research/`: comments, photos, feature ideas, maps and counting countries (21 Nov, by a research agent; all suggestions not decided).
- `site/`: the app (`index.html`, `site.css`, `js/app.js` pages and router, `js/map.js` dotted map, `js/icons.js` Lucide). Pages: home, trips by year, a trip, a post (with comments), map, countries tracker, a country, stats, Write (only on her PC: localhost/file).
- `tests/templates.js`: screenshots every Italy template post at laptop and phone into `for-jemma/templates/`, fails on sideways scrolling or a marker showing as text.
- `tests/banner_widths.js`: round-4 shots (banner at 1600/1366/1100/900/390 wide, checks the title never sits on the map; sidebar × background); `tools/make_sheets4.py` makes the sheets.
- `tests/variants.js`: screenshots of the round-3 variations into `for-jemma/looks-3/`; `tools/make_sheets3.py` makes the round-3 sheets. `tests/element_shot.js`: one element of a page (set MSYS_NO_PATHCONV=1 in Git Bash so `#/` isn’t mangled).
- `tests/shoot.js`: screenshots every look × page × laptop/phone into `for-jemma/looks/` and fails on page errors or sideways scrolling. `tools/make_sheets.py` turns them into the labelled `for-jemma/choice-*.jpg`.
