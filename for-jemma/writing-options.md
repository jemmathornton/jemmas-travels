# Ways to write posts and add photos

You asked for a few options. All of them save posts the same way (one small text file per post, photos in a folder), so you can mix them and change your mind later. None needs a sign-in online: the online site only shows posts, it never takes them in.

## 1. Through me, here in Claude

You write however you like (Notes app, Word, a messy list, a few lines from your phone) and either paste it in or tell me where it is, plus the folder of photos. I make the post from it.

- Your words stay exactly as you wrote them; I only split them into days and posts. Spelling fixes only if you ask.
- I copy the photos (never touch the originals), shrink them for the web, and take out the hidden location data so photos taken at home don’t give your address away.
- The photos’ own dates and places can sort them into the right days and put pins on the map. I’d show you what I matched before saving.
- Good for catching up on the 11 trips you already have.
- Down side: you need a chat with me each time.

## 2. A Write page on your laptop only

The Write page in the screenshots (look A, “Write”). Pick the trip, type the day or days, write, drop in photos, Save. It only exists on your own computer (like the Library’s Add a book page), so there’s nothing to sign in to and nothing anyone else can use.

- Works without me, any time.
- Shows the post as it will look straight away.
- Posts go online the next time the site is published (could be automatic, like the Library’s weekly backup, or a Publish button).

## 3. A drop folder

A folder, say `Documents\JemmasJournals\travels\new-posts`. For each post you put in the photos and a Notepad file with your words. Your laptop’s little server notices them within a few minutes and turns them into a post (the Library already does this with StoryGraph exports from Downloads).

- Nothing to open: just save files.
- If the folder is in OneDrive, you could even add notes and photos from your phone while you’re away.
- Down side: you don’t see the post until you look at the site, and file names have to follow a simple pattern.

## My suggestion (not decided)

**1 + 2.** Use me to load the past trips (lots of photos, sorting by date), and the Write page for new trips as you go. Option 3 can be added later if you want to post from your phone while travelling.
